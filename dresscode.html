Welcome Soirée

Friday, 27 August · Cocktail attire

Think summer evening in Provence: light suits or linen blazers, and cocktail dresses or smart jumpsuits. Breathable fabrics are your friend — it will still be warm after sunset.

Wedding ceremony and reception

Saturday, 28 August · Cocktail attire

Suits (ties optional) and midi or cocktail dresses. Light colours and linen are perfect for the heat. The ceremony is outdoors on grass, so block heels, wedges or smart flats rather than stilettos — and maybe a wrap for later in the evening.

Post-Wedding Pool Party

Sunday, 29 August

Wear white or a light-coloured bathing suit, plus whatever you like to throw over it. Bring sunscreen and sunglasses!
