St Tropez

We recommend taking the boat over from Sainte-Maxime and keeping an eye out for all the mega yachts in the harbour when you arrive.

Attraction

Antibes

We recommend walking the historic ramparts along the sea, then wandering the old town and the Provençal market.

Attraction

Gorges du Verdon

Europe’s answer to the Grand Canyon, about an hour and a quarter north of Seillans. Hire a pedal boat or kayak on the Lac de Sainte-Croix and paddle up into the gorge, or drive the Route des Crêtes for the views.

Attraction

Wine tasting

Some of our favourite rosé estates nearby: Château des Selves (our venue!), Château d’Esclans (home of Whispering Angel), Château Sainte Roseline and Château de Berne.

Attraction

Cannes

Stroll the Croisette, climb up to Le Suquet (the old town) for views over the bay, and take the short ferry to the Îles de Lérins for a swim and a picnic.

Attraction

Grasse

The perfume capital of the world. Tour the Fragonard perfumery, visit the International Perfume Museum, or book a workshop to make your own scent.

Attraction

Château La Coste

Cool art and good wine and olive oil. An art and architecture walk through the vineyards near Aix-en-Provence, with lovely lunch spots.

Attraction

Mougins

The hilltop village where Picasso spent his final years — full of galleries, the Musée d’Art Classique and great restaurants.
