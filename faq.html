Q&A

If you’ve got questions, have a look at our Q&A first!

When’s the RSVP deadline?

Please RSVP by 15 April so we can get an accurate headcount. 🙂

Can I bring a date?

Check your invite to see if you’ve got a plus one.

Are children welcome?

We adore your little ones, but the ceremony and reception are for adults only. That said, we know some of you will be travelling with children, so they’re more than welcome at the Friday cocktail reception and Sunday brunch! We’ll also have babysitting available at the venue during the ceremony if you need it. If you’ve got any questions, just let us know!

Where can I park?

There’s plenty of free parking near the venue entrance.

Are the ceremony and reception spaces wheelchair accessible?

Yes! We’ll also have a buggy on hand for anyone who needs a lift around the venue.

What should I wear?

Have a look at our Dresscode page and the Timetable for details for each event.

What shoes should I wear (or avoid)?

Ladies, best to give the stilettos a miss—honestly! There’s plenty of grass and uneven ground, so block heels or sandals are a safer bet. We’d hate for anyone to ruin their shoes or turn an ankle.

Who should I get in touch with if I have questions?

If you’ve got any questions, just get in touch with us directly.

What’s the weather going to be like?

Late August in Provence is hot and sunny — usually around 28–33°C in the daytime, cooling to around 18°C in the evening. Rain is rare, though the odd summer thunderstorm can roll in. Bring sunscreen, sunglasses and a hat, plus a light layer for the evenings.

Is the wedding inside or outside?

Our ceremony will be outdoors, with cocktail hour and dinner inside.

Can we take photos on our phones and cameras during the reception?

Absolutely! We’d love you to snap some photos and share them — just kindly hold off during the ceremony itself.
