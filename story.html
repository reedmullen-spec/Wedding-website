Our Story

How did you two meet?

The first time I saw him, we were both sat at the same gate waiting for a flight to Paris. I remember glancing over and thinking, Oh, he’s rather fit. When I got to my seat, I noticed the one next to me was empty and thought, wouldn’t it be brilliant if he ended up there?

Well, he did. We got chatting, he asked for my number, and then we went our separate ways. I was sure I’d never hear from him again. I thought about him now and then, but eventually he became just another ‘missed connection’. Then, a few months later, out of the blue, he messaged me saying ‘Happy New Year.’ We picked up as if nothing had changed.

Who made the first move? And how?

He made the first move by texting me on New Year's Day. A few hours later, we’d already sorted dinner plans for that week.

Who takes out the bin?

Whoever’s nearest the bin :)

What’s your most memorable date?

One of our first dates was cycling and then kayaking round the coves near Cassis. Our guide joked that a few hours in a tandem kayak would test any relationship—and he was right! But we made it, and that adventure is still one of our favourite memories.

What’s your favourite thing to do together?

We love paddleboarding and cycling in the park!

Where are you off to for your honeymoon?

Anywhere with stunning beaches! Maybe the Maldives—before it’s completely underwater?

What’s the best meal you’ve shared together?

We treated ourselves to a five-course dinner in Paris. It was absolutely magical.

What’s the most memorable trip you’ve been on together?

Last April, we saw the Northern Lights in Norway. Unforgettable!
