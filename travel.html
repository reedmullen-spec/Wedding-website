Travel

Getting here

We recommend flying into Nice Côte d’Azur Airport (about a 1 hour drive from Seillans). There are direct flights from London and New York.

Getting into the countryside

Unfortunately, getting to Seillans isn't the easiest, but it's worth it when you get here, I promise.

The best option is to rent a car (buddy up with some other guests and rent together). If not, it is possible to get here by Uber (or even a bus if you go via Cannes, but this can take around 4 hours). Keep in mind that once you're here there are no Ubers and few local taxis, so a car really is a must!

Peak season in Provence

Summer is particularly busy at Nice airport, so we’d recommend booking flights and car hire sooner rather than later to avoid ££/$$.
