Recommended neighbourhoods

If you’d like to stay together as a group, enjoy a bit more independence, or make a longer break of it, have a look at the best areas near our venue.

💑 Perfect for couples

1. Seillans

0.6 mi from the venue · 13 min walk

  • Shortest commute to the venue — many properties within a 5–10 minute drive or walk.
  • Authentic historic village atmosphere with cafés and village shops.
  • Good choice for guests wanting walkable evenings and a photogenic location.

🏡 Ideal for families

2. Fayence

6.1 mi from the venue · 17 min drive

  • Varied accommodation — village flats, country villas and family homes.
  • Good balance of local shops, restaurants and quiet residential streets.
  • Well connected by local roads; pleasant countryside for walkers and cyclists.

🏡 Ideal for families

3. Lac de Saint-Cassien

10 mi from the venue · 21 min drive

  • Scenic lakeside setting with walking, swimming and fishing.
  • Wide selection of villas and countryside homes — good for groups and families.
  • Well suited to guests wanting relaxed evenings and private outdoor space.

💑 Perfect for couples

4. Fréjus

20.2 mi from the venue · 42 min drive

  • Beaches, restaurants and a seaside atmosphere — great if you’re extending your stay.
  • Larger supply of holiday apartments and villas suitable for groups.
  • Direct road connections make it a straightforward 45–60 minute drive to the venue.
